# Qtangl GTM — Top 100 Targets (Banks · Credit Unions · Payment Processors)

**Purpose:** Outbound prospect list for Q-Day Assessment → Monitor.  
**ICP:** $25K–$250K budget; CISO / VP IT Security / compliance lead; rich external TLS + APIs.  
**Data as of:** NCUA / FDIC Q4 2025 call reports ([PlainBankData](https://plainbankdata.com/rankings/largest-banks), [CUScore](https://www.cuscore.org/rankings/)).

**Priority key**

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **P1** | First 20 outbound — regional bank/CU sweet spot, digital/API surface, fast procurement |
| **P2** | Wave 2 — strong fit, slightly larger or longer cycle |
| **P3** | Wave 3 — good fit; specialty, foreign-owned US bank, or larger CU |

**Excluded from bank list:** Big 4, money-center nationals (>$300B), investment/custody banks, foreign-charter branches.
