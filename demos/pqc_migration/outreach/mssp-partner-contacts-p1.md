**Confidence**

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| ✅ **Verified** | Company leadership page, press release, or speaker bio |
| ⚠️ **Likely** | LinkedIn + strong third-party corroboration |
| 🔍 **Needs verify** | Merger/rebrand, no channel title, or company identity unclear |

**Outreach role key**

| Role | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Channel / Alliances** | Dedicated partner program or OEM motion |
| **Growth / Commercial** | No VP Channel — owns GTM + partner community |
| **CEO / Founder** | Small MSSP; signs pilot SOW directly |
| **Parent company** | Post-acquisition; route via holding co channel desk |

**Do not cold-email guessed addresses.** Use LinkedIn InMail or partner intake forms on company sites.
