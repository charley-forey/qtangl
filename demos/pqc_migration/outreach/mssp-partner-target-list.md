# MSSP & Channel Partner Target List — Qtangl

**Purpose:** Partners who **distribute** Q-Day Assess/Monitor to banks, credit unions, and payment processors — not competitors on discovery/SOC.  
**Motion:** White-label Monitor · Readiness Passport in audit packs · 20–30% rev-share ([15-partnerships-and-ecosystem.md](../../../roadmap/quantum-readiness/15-partnerships-and-ecosystem.md))  
**Enablement:** [mssp-verify-kit.md](../../../roadmap/quantum-readiness/sales-enablement/mssp-verify-kit.md)

**Priority**

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **P1** | FinServ-only or CUSO — call first; fastest path to first rev-share SOW |
| **P2** | Strong banking/CU vertical — wave 2 |
| **P3** | National MSSP / SI — longer cycle; channel program required |
| **Audit** | Not MSSPs — auditor referral track (parallel, not white-label SOC) |

**Partner pitch (one line):** *You own SOC and exam workflows; Qtangl is the cryptographic evidence layer — CBOM, Mosca HNDL, signed `/verify` links auditors can check offline.*

---

## How many partners you need

| Horizon | Target | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| **Now → 6 mo** | **2–3 signed MSSP pilots** | Covers community banks/CUs you can't reach direct |
| **6–12 mo** | **8–12 active partners** | ~20% of pipeline from channel (per roadmap metric) |
| **12–18 mo** | **15–20 partners** | Regional coverage without hiring AEs in every market |

You do **not** need 100 MSSPs — you need **depth in FinServ** and **geographic coverage** through a few strong partners per region.

## Outreach order (first 90 days)

### Week 1–4: Sign 1 pilot (pick 2 targets)

1. **DefenseStorm** — "crypto evidence layer for your 200+ FI customers"  
2. **Locknet** OR **Think\|Stack** — Midwest/CU density  

### Week 4–8: Enable + second pilot

3. **Securafy** or **TorchLight** — different segment (bank vs CU)  
4. Send [mssp-verify-kit.md](../../../roadmap/quantum-readiness/sales-enablement/mssp-verify-kit.md) + live dogfood verify link

### Week 8–12: Channel scale

5. **STIG** + **BlueRadius** — regional bank intros  
6. Apply to **Arctic Wolf** / **Deepwatch** partner portals (P3)
