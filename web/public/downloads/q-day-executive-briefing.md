## 2. Mosca's inequality — why start now

**X + Y > Z**

| Variable | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **X** | How long sensitive data must stay confidential |
| **Y** | Time required to migrate crypto |
| **Z** | When adversaries can break today's public-key crypto |

For long-lived secrets, TLS certificates, and archived ciphertext, exposure today is liability tomorrow. Waiting compresses migration runway and raises cost.
