## Decision tree

```
NSF Pitch submitted
    ├─ Invited → Full proposal → Phase I ($275K scale)
    │       └─ Phase II → ($1M+)
    └─ Declined → Revise → AFWERX primary path
            └─ Open Topic Phase I ($75K)
                    └─ Customer Memorandum → Phase II ($1.25M)
                            └─ STRATFI (gov submits, $3M–$15M)
```
