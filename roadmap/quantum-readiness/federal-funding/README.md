# Federal Funding & Grants — Resource Hub

**Purpose:** Non-dilutive funding, federal contracts, and credibility programs to validate Qtangl's Cryptographic Posture Management (CPM) platform and fund Assess → Monitor → Convert development.

**Strategic framing:** Lead with **cybersecurity modernization, cryptographic visibility, supply chain security, and critical infrastructure protection** — not "quantum computing." The policy tailwind is NIST/CISA/NSA mandates for **cryptographic inventory, discovery, risk assessment, and migration planning** — exactly what Qtangl builds.

## Fastest path (first three moves)

1. **NSF Project Pitch** — lowest friction; SAM not required to start  
   https://seedfund.nsf.gov/project-pitch/
2. **NIST NCCoE PQC Community of Interest** — credibility multiplier (free)  
   https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/applied-cryptography/migration-to-pqc
3. **AFWERX Open Topic via DSIP** — DoD pilot path  
   https://afwerx.com/divisions/sbir-sttr/open-topic/ · https://www.dodsbirsttr.mil/submissions/login
