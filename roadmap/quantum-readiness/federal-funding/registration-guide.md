# Federal Funding — Registration Guide

Step-by-step instructions for every portal Qtangl needs before applying for SBIR/STTR grants or federal contracts.

**Estimated total time:** 2–4 weeks (SAM is the long pole). Start SAM on Day 1.

**Free help:** [APEX Accelerators](https://www.apexaccelerators.us) — schedule a session before starting SAM.

---

## Before you begin — gather these documents

| Document | Where to find |
|----------|---------------|
| Legal business name (exactly as incorporated) | Articles of incorporation |
| Physical business address (no P.O. box for primary) | Lease / utility bill |
| EIN (Employer Identification Number) | IRS CP 575 or SS-4 |
| Bank routing + account number (for ACH) | Business bank account |
| NAICS codes | Pick codes for cybersecurity software/services (e.g., 541511, 541512, 518210) |
| Ownership breakdown (% U.S. citizens/PRs) | Cap table |
| Points of contact (CEO, CFO, contracts) | Internal |
| DUNS → UEI | UEI replaces DUNS; assigned during SAM |

## 1. Login.gov

**URL:** https://login.gov  
**Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cost:** Free

Login.gov is the identity provider for SAM.gov, DSIP, and Grants.gov.

### Steps

1. Go to https://login.gov
2. Create account with email you'll use for all federal portals
3. Set up multi-factor authentication (required)
4. Use the **same email** across SAM, DSIP, Grants.gov where possible

---

## 2. SAM.gov entity registration

**URL:** https://sam.gov/entity-registration  
**Time:** 7–15 business days for activation  
**Cost:** Free (never pay third-party "SAM registration" services)

SAM registration is required to **receive** federal awards. Without it, you cannot get paid.

### What you get

- **UEI** (Unique Entity Identifier) — 12-character alphanumeric ID
- **CAGE code** (if applicable for DoD)
- Eligibility to bid on contracts and receive grants

### Steps

1. Sign in to SAM.gov with Login.gov
2. Select **Register Entity**
3. Complete sections in order:
   - **Core Data** — legal name, address, EIN, entity structure
   - **Assertions** — business type, size, NAICS codes
   - **Reps & Certs** — FAR representations (read carefully; legal attestations)
   - **Points of Contact** — Electronic Business POC, Government Business POC
   - **SBA supplemental page** (if small business NAICS selected)
4. Submit and wait for validation
5. Set calendar reminder: **renew every 365 days**

### Common mistakes

- Name mismatch between incorporation docs and SAM entry
- Wrong NAICS codes (affects small business set-aside eligibility)
- Incomplete Reps & Certs (blocks activation)
- Using personal bank account instead of business account

### Checklist

- [ ] Login.gov account created
- [ ] Entity registration submitted
- [ ] UEI received
- [ ] Registration status = Active
- [ ] Renewal date noted

## 3. SBA Company Registry (SBIR/STTR)

**URL:** https://www.sbir.gov/registration2  
**Time:** ~10 minutes  
**Cost:** Free

**Separate from SAM.** Required for every SBIR/STTR application across all agencies.

### What you get

- **SBC Control ID**
- **Registration PDF** (upload to proposal systems)

### Steps

1. Go to https://www.sbir.gov/registration2
2. Create account (can use same email as other portals)
3. Enter:
   - UEI (from SAM — can start SBA registry once UEI assigned)
   - EIN
   - Company name, address
   - Number of employees
   - Ownership details (% U.S. citizens/PRs)
   - Points of contact (CEO, CFO, Business Official, Contracting Official)
4. Submit registration
5. Download **SBC Registration PDF** from Dashboard → My Documents

### Notes

- SAM registration not strictly required to *start* SBA registry, but you need UEI
- Keep registration PDF current — proposals require it
- FAQ: https://www.sbir.gov/faq/company-registry

### Checklist

- [ ] SBA Company Registry account created
- [ ] SBC Control ID received
- [ ] Registration PDF downloaded and filed

## 4. Grants.gov

**URL:** https://www.grants.gov  
**Time:** 1–2 weeks (includes org registration + EBIZ POC confirmation)  
**Cost:** Free

Required for:
- NSF full SBIR proposals (after Project Pitch invite)
- DOE SBIR/STTR grant applications

### Steps

1. Create individual account at Grants.gov
2. **Register your organization:**
   - Requires active SAM registration (UEI)
   - Designate Authorized Organization Representative (AOR)
   - EBIZ POC at your org must confirm via email (can take several days)
3. Install **Adobe Reader** or compatible software for grant package forms
4. For DOE: allow **8 weeks** before first submission if never used Grants.gov

### Checklist

- [ ] Individual account created
- [ ] Organization registered and confirmed
- [ ] AOR designated
- [ ] Test login successful

## 5. Research.gov (NSF)

**URL:** https://www.research.gov  
**Time:** ~1 week  
**Cost:** Free

Required only after NSF Project Pitch invitation for full Phase I proposal.

### Steps

1. Create account at Research.gov
2. Link organization using UEI from SAM
3. Add PI and senior personnel
4. Familiarize with proposal submission workflow before deadline

### Notes

- Not needed for initial Project Pitch
- NSF solicitation NSF 26-510: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/small-business-innovation-research-small-business-technology/nsf26-510/solicitation

### Checklist

- [ ] Research.gov account created
- [ ] Organization linked (after SAM active)
- [ ] PI profile complete

## 6. DSIP (Defense SBIR/STTR Innovation Portal)

**URL:** https://www.dodsbirsttr.mil/submissions/login  
**Time:** 1–2 days  
**Cost:** Free

Required for all DoD SBIR/STTR proposals including AFWERX Open Topic.

### Steps

1. Sign in with Login.gov
2. First-time users: register your Small Business Concern (SBC)
3. Enter firm profile:
   - UEI from SAM
   - Company addresses and POCs
   - SBA Company Registry info
4. Add additional users if needed (PI, proposal writers)
5. Designate **Corporate Official (CO)** — only CO can certify and submit proposals
6. Browse topics: https://www.defensesbirsttr.mil/SBIR-STTR/Opportunities/
7. Review Learning & Support materials in DSIP before first submission

### Proposal submission rules

- Submit **early** — do not wait until deadline day
- All volumes must be complete + CO certification
- "In Progress" status = NOT submitted
- Read Component-Specific Instructions for each topic

### Checklist

- [ ] DSIP firm registered
- [ ] Corporate Official designated
- [ ] PI added to firm
- [ ] Reviewed DSIP help videos

## 7. DHS SBIR portal

**URL:** https://sbir.dhs.gov/sbir/public  
**Time:** 1 day  
**Cost:** Free

### Steps

1. Create account on DHS SBIR portal
2. Enter TIN/EIN, business name, UEI
3. Sign up for SBIR mailing list (bottom of portal page)
4. Validate account before solicitation deadline

### Support

- Phone: (571) 446-4869 (Mon–Fri 9am–5pm ET)
- Email: OIPPortalHelpDesk@hq.dhs.gov

### Checklist

- [ ] DHS portal account created and validated
- [ ] Mailing list subscribed

## 8. PAMS (DOE Portfolio Analysis and Management System)

**URL:** https://pamspublic.science.energy.gov  
**Time:** 1–2 days  
**Cost:** Free

Required for DOE SBIR/STTR Letter of Intent and grant processing.

### Steps (two-step process)

1. **Create individual account** at PAMS login page
2. **Register to institution:**
   - If new company: click "Create Institution"
   - Associate your profile with the institution
3. Submit LOI through PAMS when FOA opens
4. After Grants.gov submission, "register" to your application in PAMS

### Help

- Email: sc.pams-helpdesk@science.doe.gov
- Phone: (855) 818-1846
- Guide: https://pamsexternalhelp.science.energy.gov/display/ST/PAMS+Registration+Guide

### Checklist

- [ ] PAMS individual account created
- [ ] Institution created and linked
- [ ] PI associated with institution

```
Day 1:   Login.gov + start SAM.gov
```

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Action |
|---------|--------|
| SAM stuck in "Submitted" >15 days | Call SAM help desk; check Reps & Certs completeness |
| Grants.gov org not confirming | Contact EBIZ POC; check spam folder |
| DSIP can't find UEI | Verify SAM is Active; UEI exactly as shown in SAM |
| DHS portal validation fails | Ensure TIN/EIN matches SAM exactly |
| "Not small business" on SAM | Review NAICS size standards; SBA profile |
