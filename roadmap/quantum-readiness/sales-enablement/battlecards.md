# Battlecards

Quick-reference competitive cards for sales conversations. Pair with [11-competitive-intelligence.md](../11-competitive-intelligence.md) (full teardowns) and [objection-handling.md](./objection-handling.md).

**Golden rule:** Never disparage. Acknowledge competitor strength, then pivot to our differentiated value (speed + signed evidence + drift + mid-market price + honest framing).

---

## One-line positioning

> Qtangl is the post-quantum readiness platform: assess your quantum-vulnerable crypto in minutes, monitor drift continuously, and convert with signed evidence your auditors can verify — at mid-market price.

## Discovery cheat sheet (qualify)

| Question | Signal |
|----------|--------|
| "What's driving PQC interest now?" | Board/audit/contract trigger = hot |
| "Do you have a central crypto inventory?" | "No/spreadsheet" = strong fit |
| "Who owns the migration program?" | Identifies champion (VP Eng) |
| "Any 2027/2030 deadlines (NSM-10/CMMC)?" | Urgency + vertical |
| "How do you prove progress to auditors today?" | Evidence pain → our moat |
