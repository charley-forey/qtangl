# Battlecards

Quick-reference competitive cards for sales conversations. Pair with [11-competitive-intelligence.md](../11-competitive-intelligence.md) (full teardowns) and [objection-handling.md](./objection-handling.md).

**Golden rule:** Never disparage. Acknowledge competitor strength, then pivot to our differentiated value (speed + **signed, publicly-verifiable evidence** + drift + mid-market price + honest framing).

> Refreshed 2026-06-09. **Drift + remediation = Yes** on comparison matrix. **Public comparison hub:** [https://www.qtangl.com/compare](https://www.qtangl.com/compare).

## One-line positioning

> Qtangl is the post-quantum readiness platform: assess your quantum-vulnerable crypto in minutes, monitor drift continuously, and convert with **signed evidence your auditors can independently verify** — at mid-market price.

**The one slide to remember:** the "fast scan + CBOM + Mosca" core is now table stakes (several vendors do it). We win on **packaging**: public verifiable evidence + self-serve + transparent price + honesty. We sell a **fast external baseline**, not full-estate coverage — be honest about depth.

## Discovery cheat sheet (qualify)

| Question | Signal |
|----------|--------|
| "What's driving PQC interest now?" | Board/audit/contract trigger = hot |
| "Do you have a central crypto inventory?" | "No/spreadsheet" = strong fit |
| "Do you already run a crypto-discovery tool (Keyfactor, SandboxAQ, Fortanix, IBM, Palo Alto)?" | Identifies incumbent → position as fast agentless baseline + verifiable evidence layer |
| "Agent-based or agentless preference?" | Agentless preference / no rollout appetite = our lane |
| "Who owns the migration program?" | Identifies champion (VP Eng) |
| "Any 2027/2030 deadlines (NSM-10/CMMC)?" | Urgency + vertical |
| "How do you prove progress to auditors today?" | Evidence pain → our moat |
